15 Puzzle X — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Developer: megasoft1978 (it.megasoft78.puzzle15x)

What the game itself collects

15 Puzzle X does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information on our own servers. Game progress, settings and purchases are stored on your device and in your store account.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including a banner, optional rewarded videos, and occasional interstitials. The game requests non-personalized ads only (npa=1) and, where required, asks for your consent through Google's consent platform before ads load. AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can change or withdraw consent at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy. Purchasing Premium removes all ads.

Analytics

With your consent, the game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example levels played and completed) to GameAnalytics to help us improve difficulty and content. Analytics stays off unless you consent; declining changes nothing else. Policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The store provides us only the purchase state needed to unlock the content.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. Non-personalized ads and consent-gated analytics are used so that no profile is built on any player.

Data you can control

  • Ads consent: Settings → Privacy (UMP privacy options).
  • Analytics: only starts after consent; can be withdrawn the same way.
  • On-device data: uninstalling removes all local progress.

Contact

Questions: via the developer contact on the store listing.
