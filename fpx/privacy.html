15 Puzzle X — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Publisher: Antonietta Rizzo (megasoft78) · Apps: it.megasoft78.puzzle15x (Android), it.megasoft78.fifteenpuzzlex (iOS)

Who may use the app

15 Puzzle X is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

What stays on your device

The app does not require an account and has no server of ours that receives game data. Progress, settings and your analytics choice are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens, full-screen ads after some wins (capped), and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch. Purchasing Premium removes all ads.

  • Ad requests are always non-personalised (AdMob option npa=1). The app does not build an advertising profile.
  • AdMob may still process your device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, ad data, your interactions with the app, and diagnostic, performance and crash data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Android: the advertising ID (AD_ID) is available to AdMob.
  • iPhone and iPad: before ads start, the app asks once for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow tracking, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to AdMob. If you deny it, no IDFA is read.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form from Google's consent platform appears before ads load. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy.

Analytics (GameAnalytics)

Analytics is optional. The game shows its own analytics card and nothing at all is sent to GameAnalytics unless you press Accept on it. Until you answer, the card is shown again when you start the app. Your answer to Google's advertising consent form does not turn analytics on. If you refused consent in Google's form where it applies, analytics stays off even if you accept on the card.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. If you decline there, nothing more is sent and data not yet sent is discarded.

Only after you accept, the app sends to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics privacy notice):

  • a random identifier generated for your installation (a UUID, kept on the device across sessions) and a session identifier and session number;
  • device model, device manufacturer, operating system version and app build;
  • session start and end;
  • gameplay events: your acceptance, puzzles started and solved (with board size), hints shown (and how they were obtained), undo (with the move count), changes of theme and language, coins earned and spent, coins doubled by a rewarded ad (amounts), and streak freezes earned, bought or used, and that you bought Premium;
  • Android only: advertising events (such as ad requested, loaded, failed to load, shown or clicked, with error details) and ad revenue.

As with any internet connection, GameAnalytics receives your IP address each time the app connects to it.

The identifier is not your name or account, but it is persistent, so this data is personal data under privacy law and is not anonymous.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The store gives the app only the purchase state needed to unlock Premium. If you accepted analytics, the app also records for GameAnalytics that Premium was bought (no price, receipt or payment details).

Retention

Data processed by Google and GameAnalytics is kept according to their own policies:

  • Google: privacy policy and how Google retains data.
  • GameAnalytics: privacy notice and data retention and deletion policy.

Your choices and requests

  • Ads and analytics: Settings > Privacy in the app. Restore a purchase: Settings > Restore, or the Premium screen.
  • Delete on-device data: uninstall the app.
  • Request deletion of data held by AdMob or GameAnalytics: email megasoft78@yahoo.it with the subject "15 Puzzle X data request", your device model and approximate dates of play. The app does not show its analytics identifier, so include what you can.
