Pile Theme — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Publisher: Antonietta Rizzo (megasoft78) · Apps: it.megasoft78.piletheme (Android and iOS)

Who may use the app

Pile Theme is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

What stays on your device

The app does not require an account and has no server of ours that receives game data. Progress, coins, streak, settings and your analytics choice are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the screens outside the puzzle board and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a free hint. The app does not show full-screen ads between levels. Purchasing "Remove ads" hides the banner.

  • Ad requests are non-personalised. The app does not build an advertising profile and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission on iPhone or iPad.
  • AdMob may still process your device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, ad data, your interactions with ads, and diagnostic and crash data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Android: the advertising ID (AD_ID) is available to AdMob.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), a consent form from Google's consent platform appears before ads load. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.

Analytics (GameAnalytics)

Analytics is optional. The game shows its own analytics card and nothing is sent to GameAnalytics unless you press Accept on it. Until you answer, the card is shown again when you start the app. Your answer to Google's advertising consent form does not turn analytics on, and if you refused consent in Google's form where it applies, analytics stays off. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options; if you decline there, nothing more is sent.

After you accept, the app sends to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics privacy notice):

  • a random identifier generated for your installation, and session data (session start and end, session number);
  • device model, device manufacturer, operating system version and app build;
  • gameplay events, such as puzzles started and solved, hints used, coins earned and spent, streak, and that you bought a product.

As with any internet connection, GameAnalytics receives your IP address each time the app connects to it. The identifier is not your name or account, but it is persistent, so this data is personal data under privacy law and is not anonymous.

Purchases

Coin packs, the starter pack and "Remove ads" are one-time in-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The store gives the app only the purchase state needed to deliver the purchase. Use Restore purchases in the Shop to get back items you already own.

Retention

Data processed by Google and GameAnalytics is kept according to their own policies:

  • Google: privacy policy and how Google retains data.
  • GameAnalytics: privacy notice and data retention and deletion policy.

Your choices and requests

  • Ads and analytics: Settings > Privacy options in the app. Restore a purchase: Shop > Restore purchases.
  • Delete on-device data: uninstall the app.
  • Request deletion of data held by AdMob or GameAnalytics: email megasoft78@yahoo.it with the subject "Pile Theme data request", your device model and approximate dates of play.
